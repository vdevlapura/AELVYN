About AelvynAbout Aelvyn, Dubai, October 2026 Dubai, United Arab Emirates October 2026

Four agents for the back office, built in Dubai.

This page records what Aelvyn is building, why we build it in the UAE, how the agents work and where the company stands, as of October 2026.

01

What we are building.

Finance, sales, support and reporting, for businesses of 5 to 200 people.

Aelvyn builds AI agents for the everyday operations of small and mid-sized businesses in the UAE and GCC. Four areas: finance operations, which reads supplier invoices and prepares draft bills; sales operations, which captures leads and keeps the CRM current; customer support, which answers common questions in English and Arabic from your own documents; and reporting, which writes the daily and weekly numbers in plain language.

Each agent prepares work. A person approves what matters.

02

Why the UAE.

Languages
English and Arabic, as invoices here arrive in both
TRN
The 15-digit Tax Registration Number, read on every invoice
VAT
5%, checked line by line
Channels
Email and WhatsApp, as customers here write on both
Hosting
Built on AWS, encrypted in transit and at rest, designing for in-region hosting in the UAE

None of this is an edge case here. It is the first line of every workflow, so we build around it from the start, not as a translation afterwards.

03

How we work.

The same rule for every agent we build, from the first invoice onward.

Approval is the default. Anything that moves money, changes a financial record or messages a customer waits for a person. Every action is written to a log you can read.

You decide, workflow by workflow, what an agent may do on its own, and you can change that at any time.

04

Where we stand.

We are opening early access to a small group of UAE businesses. No customers, no public pricing and no certifications yet.
