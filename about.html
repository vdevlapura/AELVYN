About us

We give growing businesses an AI workforce.

Aelvyn is an early-stage AI company based in Dubai, founded in 2026.

Why we started Aelvyn

Walk into almost any growing business in the UAE and you will find capable people spending their days on work that does not need them: copying invoice details into accounting software, chasing payments, answering the same customer questions on WhatsApp, and stitching together reports from several systems.

Large enterprises solve this with operations teams, consultants and expensive automation projects. Small and mid-sized businesses usually cannot. They make up most of the economy, yet they get the least help.

We believe AI agents change that. Agents can read documents, understand messages in English and Arabic, and work across the tools a business already uses. Combined with a person who approves the steps that matter, they can take on routine operations work safely.

What we believe

  • People stay in charge. Agents prepare the work. People approve anything that touches money, customers or records.
  • Built for the region. Arabic and English, UAE VAT and local business practices are part of the product from day one.
  • Earn trust slowly. We start small with each customer, prove value on one workflow, then expand.
  • Plain and honest. We tell customers what Aelvyn can and cannot do yet.

Where we are today

We are building our first agents for finance operations and customer support, and we are opening early access to a small group of UAE businesses. Early-access customers help us decide which workflows and integrations come first.
