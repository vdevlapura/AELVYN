Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Aelvyn ("Aelvyn", "we", "us") collects, uses and protects personal information when you visit aelvyn.com or contact us.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us: your name, work email, company name, team size and any message you send when you request early access or contact us.
  • Technical information: basic data your browser sends automatically, such as browser type, device and pages visited, which our hosting provider may log for security and performance.

How we use it

  • To respond to your enquiry and manage early-access requests
  • To send product updates you have asked for (you can unsubscribe at any time)
  • To keep our website secure and improve it

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us run our business, such as cloud hosting and email providers, under appropriate confidentiality and security obligations, or where the law requires it.

Data storage and security

We use reputable cloud infrastructure, including Amazon Web Services, and industry-standard measures such as encryption in transit to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your data.

Retention

We keep your information only as long as we need it for the purposes above, or as the law requires.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your personal information, or object to how we use it, by emailing hello@aelvyn.com. We will respond in line with applicable law, including the UAE Personal Data Protection Law.

Cookies

Our website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. If this changes, we will update this policy and ask for your consent where required.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above shows when it last changed.
