1Who we are

Aelvyn is a company founded in Dubai in October 2026. Our office is Office 611, Office Court Building, Oud Metha, Dubai, United Arab Emirates. In this policy, "we" and "us" mean Aelvyn, and "you" means a visitor to aelvyn.com.

2What this policy covers

2.1This website

This policy covers aelvyn.com and the two forms on it: the early-access form on the home page and the contact form on the contact page. It also covers any email you send to hello@aelvyn.com.

2.2Not the product

Aelvyn's product is in development. It has no customers yet and is not processing data for any business. How the product will handle data is set out in a separate agreement with each early-access business before anything is connected. Clause 10 says what we can say about it today.

3What we collect

3.1What you type into a form

The early-access form asks for your full name, work email, company, team size and the area you would automate first. The contact form asks for your full name, work email, company, a topic and your message. We ask for nothing else. We never ask for payment details, identity documents or passwords through this site.

3.2What your browser sends

Like any website, aelvyn.com receives your IP address, browser type and the pages you request. Our hosting provider may keep these in server logs to keep the site secure and running. We do not combine them with your form details.

3.3What we do not collect

We run no analytics, no advertising scripts and no tracking pixels. We do not buy data about you from anyone.

4Where a form submission goes

4.1Two routes

When you submit a form, one of two things happens. If our form service is switched on, your details are sent to Web3Forms, which delivers them to hello@aelvyn.com by email. If it is not, your own email application opens with the details filled in and addressed to hello@aelvyn.com, and nothing leaves your device until you press send yourself.

4.2Where it ends up

Either way, the result is an email in our business mailbox. That mailbox is where your details live. There is no customer database behind this website.

5How we use your details

5.1To reply

We use your details to answer your message and, if you asked for early access, to arrange a short call about your workflows. We reply by email, from a person.

5.2Nothing else

We do not add you to a newsletter. We do not share your details with other companies for their marketing, and we do not sell them. If we ever want to write to you about something you did not ask about, we will ask first.

6Who else receives data

6.1Web3Forms

When our form service is switched on, Web3Forms carries your submission to our mailbox and handles it under its own privacy policy.

6.2Google Fonts

Every page loads its typefaces from Google Fonts. Your browser requests the font files from Google, which receives your IP address under Google's privacy policy.

6.3Google Maps

The contact page embeds a Google Maps frame showing our building. Loading it sends your IP address to Google and may set cookies from Google, under Google's own policies. No other page loads it.

6.4Our hosting provider

The company that serves the site's files may keep server logs as described in clause 3.2.

6.5The law

We will disclose details if a court or a competent authority in the United Arab Emirates requires it. No one else receives your details.

7Cookies and tracking

7.1This site

aelvyn.com sets no cookies of its own. It uses no analytics and no advertising technology.

7.2Google services

Google Fonts and the Google Maps frame on the contact page are third-party services and may set cookies or similar identifiers under Google's policies. You can block them in your browser. The site still reads in a fallback typeface, and our address is printed in text beside the map.

7.3If this changes

If we ever add a cookie or a script of our own, we will update this policy first and ask for your consent where the law requires it.

8Storage, security and retention

8.1Storage

Your form details are kept as email in our business mailbox, which is protected by access controls and encrypted in transit.

8.2Retention

We keep your message for as long as we need it to reply and to follow up on an early-access request. If you join early access, your details move under that agreement. If you do not, we delete the thread when you ask, and we do not keep it for any other purpose.

8.3A plain word on risk

Email is not beyond every risk, and we will not pretend otherwise. If you would rather not put something in writing, say so in your message and we will arrange a call.

9Your rights

9.1What you can ask

Under the UAE Personal Data Protection Law, Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021, and any other law that applies to you, you can ask us to tell you what we hold about you, to correct it, to delete it, or to stop using it.

9.2How to ask

Write to hello@aelvyn.com from the address you used, or tell us which address you used. We act on the request and confirm by email.

9.3If you are not satisfied

You may raise the matter with the competent data protection authority in the United Arab Emirates.

10The product, in development

10.1Where it stands

Aelvyn is building AI agents for the everyday operations of small and mid-sized businesses in the UAE and GCC. The product is in development. We are opening early access to a small group of UAE businesses. We have no customers yet.

10.2What governs it

When a business joins early access, a separate written agreement sets out what the agents may read, what they may do alone, what waits for a person, how long data is kept and where it is hosted. Nothing on this website is that agreement.

10.3Our design intent, for the record

The product is built on AWS and encrypts data in transit and at rest. We are designing for in-region hosting in the UAE. Every action an agent takes is written to a log the business can read. Anything that moves money, changes a financial record or messages a customer waits for a person to approve it.

11Changes to this policy

When this policy changes, the date at the top of the page changes with it. The current version is always the one at aelvyn.com/privacy.html. We do not change it quietly.
