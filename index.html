The problem

Growing teams are buried in manual work.

Small and mid-sized businesses run on a patchwork of email, spreadsheets, accounting software, CRMs and WhatsApp. People spend hours every week moving information between them by hand.

  • Copying invoice and order details from email into accounting tools
  • Chasing payments and leads with manual follow-ups that slip through the cracks
  • Answering the same customer questions in English and Arabic all day
  • Building weekly reports from five different systems
What Aelvyn does

One AI workforce for your everyday operations

Each Aelvyn agent owns one job, connects to the tools you already use and does that job from start to finish.

Finance operations

Reads invoices and receipts from email, enters them into your accounting system, matches payments and chases overdue accounts.

Sales operations

Captures leads from email, web forms and WhatsApp, keeps your CRM up to date and sends timely, personal follow-ups.

Customer support

Answers common questions in English and Arabic using your own policies and documents, and passes complex cases to your team.

Reporting

Sends plain-language daily and weekly summaries pulled from all your systems, and answers questions like "who owes us the most?"

FAQ

Questions we hear often

Who is Aelvyn for?

Small and mid-sized businesses in the UAE and GCC, typically 5 to 200 people, whose teams spend a lot of time on invoices, follow-ups, customer messages and reports.

Will the agents do things without my permission?

No. You decide what each agent may do on its own. Anything sensitive, such as sending a message to a customer, changing financial records or scheduling a payment, waits for your approval by default.

Is my data safe?

We build on AWS with encryption in transit and at rest, and access limited to what each agent needs. We do not sell your data or use it to train models for other customers. We are designing for in-region hosting in the UAE.

Does it work in Arabic?

Yes. English and Arabic support is a core design goal, for reading documents and for replying to customers.

Which tools does Aelvyn connect to?

We are building integrations for common email, accounting, CRM and messaging tools, including Gmail, Outlook, WhatsApp Business, Zoho Books, QuickBooks and Xero. Early-access customers help decide what comes first.

How much will it cost?

We will share pricing with early-access customers before any commitment. Our aim is simple monthly plans that cost far less than the hours they save.
