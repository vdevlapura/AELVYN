The problem

Growing teams are buried in manual work.

Small and mid-sized businesses run on a patchwork of email, spreadsheets, accounting software, CRMs and WhatsApp. People spend hours every week moving information between them by hand.

  • Copying invoice and order details from email into accounting tools
  • Chasing payments and leads with manual follow-ups that slip through the cracks
  • Answering the same customer questions in English and Arabic all day
  • Building weekly reports from five different systems
What Aelvyn does

One AI workforce for your everyday operations

Aelvyn agents connect to the tools you already use and take care of routine work from start to finish.

Finance operations

Read invoices and receipts, enter them into your accounting system and chase overdue payments.

Sales operations

Capture leads, keep your CRM up to date and send timely, personal follow-ups.

Customer support

Answer common questions in English and Arabic over email and chat, and pass complex cases to your team.

Reporting

Get plain-language daily and weekly summaries pulled from all of your systems.

How it works

From busywork to automated in four steps

1

Connect

Link your email, accounting, CRM and messaging tools securely.

2

Describe

Tell Aelvyn what you want done in plain English. No code or complex setup.

3

Approve

Agents do the work and ask for your sign-off on anything sensitive before acting.

4

Improve

Agents learn from your feedback and get more useful every week.
