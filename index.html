01

Most of the back office is copying.

A supplier emails a PDF. Someone reads it, types it into the accounting system, checks it against the order and files it. A customer asks about delivery on WhatsApp. Someone answers, then forgets to log it. The week's numbers live in five systems and one person's head.

01.1 Supplier invoice and PO check Email to accounting system, checked against the order By hand
01.2 New lead and follow-up WhatsApp to CRM, then CRM to email By hand
01.3 Delivery question and payment reminder WhatsApp and email, answered and logged By hand
01.4 Weekly numbers Five systems to a spreadsheet By hand

Every line above is typed by a person today.

Four kinds of work. Each one specific.

01.5 Finance operations

Reads supplier invoices and receipts as they arrive by email. Extracts the supplier, TRN, line items, VAT and due date. Matches the purchase order and prepares a draft bill in your accounting system. Matches payments when they land. Chases customers whose invoices are overdue.

Waits for approval: posting a bill, scheduling a payment, sending a reminder.
Pilot next
01.6 Customer support

Answers common questions in English and Arabic, using only your own documents and policies. When a case is complex, hands it to your team with a summary of what was asked and what has already been said.

Waits for approval: refunds, exceptions, anything your documents do not cover.
Pilot next
01.7 Sales operations

Captures leads from email, web forms and WhatsApp. Prepares the new or updated CRM record so nobody retypes it. Drafts the follow-up and leaves it for a person to send.

Waits for approval: any CRM change, by default, and any message to a lead.
Later
01.8 Reporting

Writes a plain-language summary every morning and every week, drawn from all your systems. Answers questions in plain words: who owes us the most, what did we spend with this supplier this quarter. Read-only. Never changes a record.

Waits for approval: nothing. It only reads.
Later

08

Questions we are asked.

08.1Will an agent act without my permission?

No. Approval is the default. You choose, per workflow, what an agent may do alone. Anything that moves money, changes a financial record or messages a customer waits for a person.

08.2Where is my data?

Nothing is connected until an early-access agreement is signed. The product is built on AWS, encrypts data in transit and at rest, and we are designing for in-region hosting in the UAE. The details are in our Privacy Policy.

08.3Does it read Arabic?

Yes. Arabic and English, for documents coming in and for replies going out.

08.4Which tools does it connect to?

Integrations for email, accounting, CRM, messaging and spreadsheets are in development. None is live yet. Early-access businesses help decide the order.

08.5What does it cost?

We have no public pricing. Early-access businesses will see pricing before any commitment.

08.6Who else uses it?

Nobody yet. We are opening early access to a small group of UAE businesses.

08.7How do I start?

Request early access below. We arrange a short call about your workflows. If Aelvyn does not fit, we say so on the call and tell you what would.
